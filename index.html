Straight answers

Questions

Where does "market rent" come from?

HUD Fair Market Rents: free public data from the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development for ZIPs in metro areas. They estimate what recent movers paid for a typical unit (rent plus utilities). It's a benchmark, not an appraisal of your apartment, and every result shows its source.

Is this legal advice?

No. STAY OR GO is not a law firm. The rules check is general information about rent caps and notice periods. For your situation, talk to a local tenant-rights group or attorney.

Will my landlord know I used this?

Only if you tell them. We never contact anyone. The messages are written in your voice for you to send.

Where is my data stored?

Only on this device. There's no account and no database. Photos you choose to scan are sent to Anthropic's Claude AI to read them and aren't kept by us. Tap "Delete my data" below to erase everything.
